Private Online Consultation

Please provide your details and medical concerns below. All consultations are handled confidentially and professionally.

Consultation Fee: R 500

The fee for the first online gynaecological consultation is R 500. Payment will be requested if an appointment is offered.

What Your Consultation Includes

Your R 500 consultation includes:

  • Review of the information submitted by you.
  • A private online video consultation.
  • Discussion of your concerns and relevant medical history.
  • Clinical assessment based on the information available during the consultation.
  • Advice regarding appropriate next steps.
  • Referral for in-person examination, investigation or specialist care where appropriate.
  • A written prescription or referral letter where clinically appropriate.

Your consultation is conducted privately and confidentially.

Urgent consultations are prioritised for faster clinical review.

Your information remains confidential and is handled with discretion.
