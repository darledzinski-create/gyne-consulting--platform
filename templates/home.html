Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an emergency service?

No. This platform is not intended for emergencies. If you require urgent medical attention, please contact emergency services or your nearest healthcare facility.

How quickly will I receive a response?

All consultations are personally reviewed by Dr Dariusz Ledzinski and responded to as promptly as possible.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Your consultation information is handled professionally and treated as confidential.

Will I receive phone calls?

No. Communication through this service is conducted primarily by email or WhatsApp, depending on the contact method you select when submitting your consultation request.

Who reviews my consultation?

All consultations are personally reviewed by Dr Dariusz Ledzinski. This is not an automated service and responses are tailored to the information you provide.

Can an appointment be arranged?

Yes. Where appropriate, an online or in-person appointment may be offered following review of your consultation.
